ABOUT

My name is Matteo Del Rossi, and I am a |


I compose music for films, games, and other media! Most recently, I composed the score for Nyle E. Rivera's original theatrical production "To Have & To Hold". If you're interested in working with me, shoot me an email!

Broadly, my passion is creation. I get great satisfaction out of engineering creative solutions to meaningful problems, and bringing something into the world which did not exist before. I satiate this desire for innovation through programming, music composition, piano improvisation, physical engineering, sketch comedy, poetry, and cooking.

I am currently a second-year undergraduate student at Arizona State University pursuing a bachelor's degree in Computer Science with a minor in both Popular Music Production and Mathematics.
