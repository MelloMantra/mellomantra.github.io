MUSIC

ABOUT

I have been making music since I first learned to play piano at age 5. I enjoyed playing, but composing was always more alluring. I would write sheet music in a little book my mom got me.

The genre of music I create changes with the seasons. I currently have several projects lined up, including a full film score and video game soundtrack.

I'm inspired by people like Rick Rubin, creating art through some sort of divine connection. I resonate with the idea that by just playing your instrument of choice and jamming, soloing until you hit a groove, is like being in sync with some higher force. I long for that transcendental feeling. Eyes closed, fingers moving as if possessed, pouring your heart onto your piano, truly an out of body experience. And then, as swiftly as they entered, the higher forces dissipate as you open your eyes.

Music available on all major streaming platforms.
